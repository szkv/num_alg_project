## Project Representation: Szozat (Hungarian Wordle) Solver & Optimization

**1. Introduction & Aim**
*   **The Game:** Wordle is a game where the player must guess a secret word within 6 chances, receiving Green, Yellow, or Black feedback to narrow down possibilities[[1]].
*   **The Concept:** Compare two existing mathematical heuristics (Greedy/Frequency and Shannon Entropy) as evaluation engines. We will use Genetic Algorithms (GA) and Simulated Annealing (SA) to navigate the search space and evaluate 4 different algorithmic combinations. [2][3][[4]]
*   **The Problem:** We will evaluate computational speed, solution quality by expected number of guess in wich we solve the game(ENG), the starting word or pair of words with the lesd ENG and the "human problem" of guaranteeing a win by fixing the first 4 to 5 words. We will compare our Hungarian (Szózat) results to existing English literature benchmarks.

**2. Breakdown of Algorithms: The Evaluation Engines (Bases)**
*   **Frequency-Based (Greedy):** A bot that eliminates the largest subsets of unrevealed words by using a global character frequency map. It scores candidate words by the sum of frequencies of unrevealed letters, prioritizing words with zero overlap to eliminate the largest chunk of the alphabet[2].
*   **Shannon Entropy (Information Theory):** A mathematical solver that groups the remaining candidate answer space into bins based on the feedback pattern (Green/Yellow/Black). It calculates the expected information gain to select the guess that minimizes the remaining search space[3].

**3. Metaheuristic Optimization**
*   **Genetic Algorithm (GA):**
    *   **Basic Concept:** The algorithm initializes a population of individuals (sequences of words), evaluates them, selects the best performing sequences (elite selection), and applies crossover and mutation to create the next generation.[[4]]
    *   **For 1-2 Words:** Randomly mutating a single word is too chaotic. Instead, the GA uses "artificial chromosomes" based on word traits (e.g., target vowel count, high-frequency consonants) or relies on 1-character Hamming mutations (changing a single letter to step to an anagram or similar valid guess).
    *   **For 4-5 Words:** The fixed block of 4-5 words acts as the "individual," and each distinct integer representing a word acts as a "chromosome" that can be swapped during crossover or mutation.
*   **Simulated Annealing (SA):**
    *   **Basic Concept:** The algorithm evaluates a single state (a word sequence) and its "energy" (average guess count). It explores the search space at a given temperature.[[4]]
    *   **Move Evaluation:** If a randomly generated next step reduces the expected guesses (negative difference in energy), it is accepted. If it is a bad move, it is accepted with a probability dependent on the temperature to escape local optima. The system then cools down over time until it crystallizes on an optimal solution.
    *   **For 1-2 Words:** The neighborhood of possible next steps is generated by randomized 1-character Hamming mutations to step to similar words.
    *   **For 4-5 Words:** The algorithm proposes moves by randomly swapping one of the words in the sequence with another valid word from the dictionary.

**4. Visualization**
*   **Local Level:** After each guess in the simulation, generate visualizations showing how the remaining viable guess pool shrinks and how the entropy drops. This will demonstrate the correlation between the Frequency and Entropy evaluators.
*   **Global Level:** Create performance histograms showing the ENG-s for different starting sequences, alongside convergence plots tracking computational time and optimization progress for the GA and SA.
